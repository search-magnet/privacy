隐私政策

本政策说明 Magnet Search Android 应用如何处理你的信息。简单说：收藏和搜索历史只保存在你的手机上， 搜索不联网，应用不包含广告或统计 SDK。

01我们保存哪些数据

Magnet Search 不需要注册账号，也不要求你提供姓名、邮箱、手机号、位置或其他身份信息。 应用只在本机保存以下偏好和操作记录：

收藏的磁力保存你点过星标的磁力 infohash，用于在“我的收藏”中展示。
搜索历史保存最近最多 20 条搜索词，去重并按时间倒序排列，用于快速回填搜索。
语言偏好保存你选择的界面语言（中文或英文）。

上述数据仅存储在本应用的 Android 私有存储（SharedPreferences）中，不会上传到我们的服务器， 也不会提供给广告商、分析服务或其他第三方。

02搜索如何工作

磁力搜索在本机内置的离线索引上执行。你输入的搜索词只用于本地数据库查询， 不会发送到搜索服务、服务器或第三方。因此搜索结果不依赖网络，飞行模式下也可以使用。

03网络请求与服务器日志

为了获取公开磁力索引的增量更新，应用会访问开发者自行运维的接口： https://yingshi.auburn.today/api/app/sync。

该请求只包含：

  • 本地已有的索引同步游标；
  • 单次返回数量上限；
  • 通用 User-Agent（应用版本标识）。

请求不包含账号、设备唯一标识、收藏内容、搜索历史或搜索词。

和绝大多数网络服务一样，服务器在收到请求时可能自动记录 IP 地址、请求时间、请求路径和 User-Agent。 这些日志仅用于服务安全、防滥用、故障排查和容量统计，不用于广告、用户画像或出售数据。

如果你通过应用打开外部网页或应用商店，目标网站会按照它自己的隐私政策处理数据，本政策不覆盖第三方网站。

04应用权限

当前版本只申请以下 Android 权限：

INTERNET用于获取公开磁力索引增量更新。
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE用于判断当前网络是否可用，避免无网络时重复请求。

应用不申请位置、通讯录、短信、相机、麦克风、照片/文件、存储或设备标识相关权限， 也不读取其他应用的数据。

05第三方 SDK 与数据共享

应用不包含广告 SDK、统计分析 SDK、崩溃上报 SDK、推送 SDK 或社交登录 SDK， 不出售、出租或交换任何用户数据。

仅在下列情况下可能披露信息：为遵守适用法律、法规或有效法律程序；为保护用户、开发者或公众的安全与权利； 或为排查严重的服务安全问题所必需。

06数据保留与删除

收藏、搜索历史和语言偏好会一直保存在本机，直到你主动处理：

  • 在“设置 → 我的收藏”中取消收藏；
  • 在“设置 → 搜索历史”中清空历史；
  • 在 Android 系统设置中清除应用数据；
  • 卸载应用。

服务器访问日志只在运维和安全所需的最短期限内保留，随后按常规日志轮转策略删除或匿名化。

07儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。由于应用不要求账号，也不收集姓名、邮箱、 位置等信息，正常情况下不会持有儿童的个人数据。如果你认为儿童信息被不当处理，请联系我们删除。

08安全

本地数据存放在 Android 应用私有目录，受系统沙箱保护；网络同步使用 HTTPS 传输。 但任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全，请妥善保管你的设备和解锁凭证。

09政策更新与联系

如果应用功能或数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页面并修改生效日期。重大变更会在应用内或商店页面提示。

01What we store

Magnet Search does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or other identity information. The app stores only the following preferences and actions on your device:

FavoritesInfohashes you starred, used to show them in “Favorites”.
Search historyUp to 20 recent search terms, de-duplicated and ordered newest first, used to refill searches.
LanguageYour selected interface language (Chinese or English).

This data is stored only in the app’s private Android storage (SharedPreferences). It is not uploaded to our servers and is not shared with advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

02How search works

Magnet search runs against an offline index bundled with the app. Your search terms are used only for local database queries and are not sent to a search service, server, or third party. Search therefore works without a network connection, including in airplane mode.

03Network requests and server logs

To receive incremental updates to the public magnet index, the app requests the developer-operated endpoint at https://yingshi.auburn.today/api/app/sync.

The request contains only:

  • the local index sync cursor;
  • the maximum number of rows to return;
  • a generic User-Agent identifying the app version.

It does not contain an account, device identifier, favorites, search history, or search terms.

As with most network services, the server may automatically log the IP address, request time, request path, and User-Agent. These logs are used only for security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and capacity planning. They are not used for advertising or user profiling, and are not sold.

If you open an external website or app store from the app, that third party handles data under its own privacy policy. This policy does not cover third-party websites.

04Permissions

The current version requests only:

INTERNETTo receive incremental updates to the public magnet index.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATETo check network availability and avoid unnecessary requests.

The app does not request location, contacts, SMS, camera, microphone, photos/files, storage, or device identifier permissions, and does not read data belonging to other apps.

05Third-party SDKs and sharing

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, push-notification, or social-login SDK. We do not sell, rent, or trade user data.

Information may be disclosed only when required to comply with applicable law or valid legal process; to protect the safety and rights of users, the developer, or the public; or to investigate a serious service security issue.

06Retention and deletion

Favorites, search history, and language preference remain on your device until you act:

  • remove a favorite in “Settings → Favorites”;
  • clear history in “Settings → Search history”;
  • clear app data in Android system settings;
  • uninstall the app.

Server access logs are retained only for the shortest period needed for operations and security, then deleted or anonymized under standard log rotation.

07Children’s privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app requires no account and collects no name, email address, or location, it does not normally hold children’s personal data. Contact us if you believe a child’s information has been handled improperly.

08Security

Local data is stored in the app’s private Android directory and protected by the operating system sandbox. Network sync uses HTTPS. No storage or transmission method is absolutely secure, so please protect your device and unlock credentials.

09Changes and contact

If app features or data practices change, we will update this page and revise the effective date. Material changes will be noted in the app or on the store listing.
